# Shipping

## Shipping costs
Standard shipping is free on US orders over $75. Orders under $75 pay a flat $6.95. Express shipping (2 business days) is $14.95 and overnight shipping is $24.95.

## Delivery times
Orders are processed within 1 business day. Standard US delivery takes 3 to 5 business days after shipping. Orders placed after 2 pm Pacific time ship the next business day.

## International shipping
We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union and Australia. International shipping is a flat $14.95 to Canada and $24.95 elsewhere, and takes 7 to 14 business days. Customs duties and taxes are paid by the customer on delivery, except for Canada where duties are included.

## Tracking your order
You will receive a tracking number by email when your order ships. You can also ask this assistant for your order status with your order number and email address.

## Delays
Carrier delays sometimes happen because of severe weather or high volume. If your order is more than 5 business days late, contact our support team and we will investigate with the carrier.

## Changing or cancelling an order
You can cancel or change an order while it is still processing, usually within 1 hour of ordering. Once an order has shipped it cannot be changed, but you can return it after delivery.
