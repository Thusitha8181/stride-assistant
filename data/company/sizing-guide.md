# Sizing Guide

## How our shoes fit
Stride shoes are true to US size for most people. Our running shoes have a roomy toe box. Boots run slightly large, so consider half a size down if you are between sizes. Kids' sizes run from 1 to 6.

## Wide fit
Many styles come in a wide (2E for men's, D for women's) fit. Wide is a good choice if standard shoes feel tight across the ball of your foot. Look for "wide" in the available widths on each product.

## Measuring your foot
Measure your foot in the evening, standing up, from the heel to the longest toe. Leave about a thumb's width (1 cm) of space in front of your longest toe. If one foot is longer, choose the size for the longer foot.

## Size conversions
US men's 9 is about UK 8.5 and EU 42.5. US men's 10 is about UK 9.5 and EU 44. US men's 11 is about UK 10.5 and EU 45. For women's sizes, subtract 1.5 from the US men's size.
