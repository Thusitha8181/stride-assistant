# About Stride Footwear

## Who we are
Stride Footwear is an independent shoe brand founded in 2012 in Portland, Oregon. We design running, trail, casual, boot, kids and sandal styles for people who spend their days on their feet. Stride is a fictional company used for this demo.

## What we make
Our range covers road running shoes (Swift, Cloudpace, Tempo Pro, Daily Jog, Stability GT), trail and hiking shoes (Trail Runner X, Trail Runner Y, Ridge Hiker Mid, Mudlark Trail), casual sneakers and slip-ons, leather and winter boots, kids' shoes and sandals. Most adult styles come in US sizes 6 to 13 in half sizes, and many come in a wide fit.

## Where to buy
You can buy Stride shoes on our website, in our four flagship stores, and through selected outdoor retailers. Prices on our website are in US dollars.
