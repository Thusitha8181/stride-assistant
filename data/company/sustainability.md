# Sustainability

## Materials
The EcoStep Sneaker is made from 70% recycled materials. Across our range, 45% of the materials we used in 2025 were recycled or bio-based. All of our boxes are made from 100% recycled cardboard.

## Take-back program
Send us any worn-out Stride shoes using a free take-back label and we will recycle them into playground surfaces. You get a $10 voucher for every pair.

## Manufacturing
Our shoes are made by audited partner factories in Vietnam and Portugal. We publish our supplier list every year.
