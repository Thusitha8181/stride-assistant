# Shoe Care

## Cleaning
Remove loose dirt with a soft brush. Clean uppers with cold water and mild soap, then air dry away from direct heat. The Everyday Knit Sneaker can go in the washing machine on a cold, gentle cycle without the insole.

## Leather and suede
Use a leather conditioner every few months on leather boots. Protect suede with a suede spray and clean it only with a suede brush.

## Waterproof shoes
StormShield waterproof shoes stay waterproof longer if you clean off mud after each use and re-apply a waterproofing spray every few months.
