# Frequently Asked Questions

## Do you offer gift cards?
Yes. Digital gift cards from $25 to $500 are available on our website and never expire. Gift cards cannot be used to buy other gift cards.

## Do you offer discounts?
Students, teachers, healthcare workers and military members get 15% off with verification on our website. Sign up to our newsletter to get 10% off your first order.

## Can I use more than one promo code?
Only one promo code can be used per order. Promo codes cannot be applied to past orders.

## Is my payment secure?
Yes. Payments are processed by a PCI-compliant payment provider. Never share your full card number in chat; this assistant will never ask for it.

## Do you price match?
We do not price match other retailers. If a Stride item drops in price within 14 days of your purchase on our website, contact support for a one-time price adjustment.
