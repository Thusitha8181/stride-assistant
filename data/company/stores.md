# Store Locations

## Portland flagship
Stride Portland, 210 NW 10th Ave, Portland, OR. Open Monday to Saturday 10 am to 8 pm, Sunday 11 am to 6 pm. Offers free gait analysis for runners.

## Seattle
Stride Seattle, 1501 4th Ave, Seattle, WA. Open every day 10 am to 7 pm.

## Denver
Stride Denver, 1600 Wynkoop St, Denver, CO. Open Monday to Saturday 10 am to 7 pm, closed Sundays. Includes a trail shoe test ramp.

## Austin
Stride Austin, 301 Congress Ave, Austin, TX. Open every day 10 am to 8 pm.

## In-store returns
Online orders can be returned or exchanged at any Stride store within 30 days of delivery. Bring your order number.
