# Warranty

## Coverage
All Stride shoes come with a 1-year warranty from the delivery date against manufacturing defects, such as sole separation, broken eyelets or seam failure. Final sale items are covered too.

## What is not covered
The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, damage from misuse, accidents, improper care, or shoes used for more than 500 miles of running.

## How to make a warranty claim
Contact our support team with your order number and photos of the defect. If the claim is approved we will replace the shoes, or issue store credit if the style is no longer available. Warranty claims usually take 3 to 5 business days to review.
