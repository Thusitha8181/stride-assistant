# Return Policy

## Return window
You can return or exchange items within 30 days of delivery. The 30 days start on the day the item is delivered, not the day it was ordered. For orders shipped in several parts, each item's window starts when that item is delivered.

## Eligibility
Returned items must be unworn, in their original condition, and in the original box. Shoes that have been worn outdoors cannot be returned, but may be covered by our warranty if they are defective. Items that have not been delivered yet cannot be returned; you can cancel an order while it is still processing.

## Final sale items
Items marked as final sale (including clearance items discounted 40% or more) cannot be returned or exchanged. Final sale items are still covered by our 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects.

## Refunds
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive and inspect your return. Original shipping charges are not refunded unless the item was defective or we made an error.

## Exchanges
Exchanges for a different size or color of the same style are free. If the size you want is out of stock, we will issue a refund instead.

## How to start a return
Start a return through this chat assistant or on the Returns page of our website with your order number and email address. You will receive a return authorization (RMA) number and a prepaid return label for US orders. International customers pay return shipping.

## Return address
Returns go to: Stride Footwear Returns Center, 4500 Industrial Way, Reno, NV 89502, USA. Always include your RMA number on the package.
